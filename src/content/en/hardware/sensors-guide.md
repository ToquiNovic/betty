---
title: Compatible Physical Sensors
description: Wiring and JSON data structures for popular environmental and motion sensors.
---

- **BME280 (I2C)**: Temperature, humidity, barometric pressure.
- **MPU6050 (I2C)**: 6-Axis accelerometer and gyroscope for real-time 3D motion tracking.
- **DHT22**: Temperature and relative humidity.
- **BH1750**: Digital ambient light intensity in Lux.
